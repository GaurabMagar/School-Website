Admission Procedure

  1. Fill out the admission form.
  2. Submit required documents.
  3. Attend entrance assessment/interview.
  4. Complete admission confirmation process.
  5. Pay admission and required fees.

Eligibility Criteria

Primary Level

Students must meet age requirements and previous academic qualifications.

Secondary Level

Students must provide previous school records and certificates.

Higher Secondary

Students must complete required SEE or equivalent qualification.

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate
  • Previous School Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Passport Size Photos
  • Parent/Guardian Identification
  • Academic Records
